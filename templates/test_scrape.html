📌 Pinterest Trends Bookmarklet

One click on any Pinterest Trends category page — automatically pulls keywords, metrics, and product names into your trend entry.

{# Step 1: Install #}
1 Install the bookmarklet (one time)

Drag the button below into your browser's bookmarks bar. If your bookmarks bar is hidden: View → Show Bookmarks Bar (⌘+Shift+B).

Click the box to copy · then paste in Console · press Enter
{# Step 2: Use it #}
2 Use it on any Pinterest Trends category page
  1. Go to trends.pinterest.com → pick your product category (e.g. Face Lotions & Creams)
  2. Scroll all the way down so everything lazy-loads
  3. Click "View all" on Search Queries to expand the full keyword list
  4. Scroll through the Top Products carousel so all product tiles load
  5. Click the 📌 Aura Trends Import bookmark in your bar
✅ A green popup appears on the Pinterest page confirming keywords saved, products found, and AI brief generated.
The matching Trends entry in your app updates automatically — no copy-paste, no terminal.
⚠️ Make sure the category already exists as an entry in your Trends tab first. The bookmarklet matches by name — if there's no entry, it'll tell you.
{# Step 3: What it grabs #}
3 What it extracts
✅ Gets from the page
  • All search query keywords (trend pills)
  • Outbound click % growth
  • Engagement % growth
  • Save % growth
  • Product pin URLs from carousel
✅ Fetches from pins
  • Full product names from each pin's JSON-LD
  • Merges with existing products (never deletes)
  • Up to 20 pins per run
✅ Then automatically
  • Merges keywords into the matching trend entry (never overwrites)
  • Merges new products (never overwrites existing)
  • Regenerates the AI brief with fresh metrics data
