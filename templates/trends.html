How to get pin URLs from Pinterest Trends ▸

1. Go to trends.pinterest.com → open a product category → scroll to Top Products

2. Press Cmd+Option+I → click Console tab

3. Paste this command and press Enter:

var ta=document.createElement('textarea');ta.value=[...new Set([...document.querySelectorAll('a[href]')].map(a=>a.href).filter(h=>h.includes('pinterest.com/pin')))].join('\n');ta.style='position:fixed;top:0;left:0;width:100%;height:200px;z-index:99999;background:white;font-size:12px;';document.body.appendChild(ta);ta.select();

4. Press Cmd+C to copy all URLs, then paste them below

Product names will be extracted automatically when you click Save Data.
